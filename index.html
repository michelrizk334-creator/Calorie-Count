Nutrition values are always entered for RAW food. If you log Raw, the app uses them directly. If you log Cooked, the app converts the cooked quantity back to its raw equivalent automatically. Example: 100 g cooked meat × 1.30 = 130 g raw equivalent; 184 g cooked pasta ÷ 1.84 = 100 g raw equivalent. Leave conversion as “No conversion” for foods where raw/cooked does not apply.
